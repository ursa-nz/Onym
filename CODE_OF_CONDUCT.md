# Code of Conduct

Onym follows the [GNOME Code of Conduct](https://conduct.gnome.org/). We adopt it in full as the
standard of behaviour in this project's spaces: the repository, issues, pull requests, and any other
channel the project uses.

## In short

- Be friendly, patient, and welcoming.
- Be considerate and respectful. Disagree well, and assume good faith.
- Choose your words with care. Harassment, discrimination, and personal attacks are not acceptable.
- When someone is harmed, listen and put it right.

The full text, with the complete standards and definitions, is at <https://conduct.gnome.org/>.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour in an Onym space, report it to the maintainer at
<code@ursa.nz>. Reports are handled confidentially and in good faith.

This code is adapted from the GNOME Code of Conduct, by the GNOME Foundation.
